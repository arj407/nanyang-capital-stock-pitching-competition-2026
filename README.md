# Nanyang Capital Stock Pitching Competition 2026

Team Red Bull's Arm Holdings (NASDAQ: ARM) short pitch for the SemiAnalysis × Nanyang Capital Stock Pitch Challenge 2026.

## Documents

- [Written report](Red%20Bull.WrittenReport.pdf)
- [Excel financial model](RedBull.FinancialModel.xlsx)

The report presents the investment thesis, operating forecasts, valuation, catalysts and risks. The workbook includes linked financial statements, bear/base/bull scenarios, DCF and reverse DCF valuations, peer comparisons, short-return calculations and reconciliation checks.

Download the Excel file and open it in Microsoft Excel to explore the model. The workbook is kept together because its worksheets, formulas and charts are linked. The scenario selector is in **Assumptions!E5**: 1 = Bear, 2 = Base, 3 = Bull.

## Research basis

The pitch uses a reference price of $227.80 at the 16 October 2026 close and a $125 target by 16 October 2027. These are historical competition assumptions, not a current investment recommendation. Sources, assumptions and the risks to the short thesis are documented in the report and workbook.
